## Brand & Style

The design system targets ambitious IGCSE and A-Level secondary students who require deep focus, rigorous academic structure, and modern motivation. The experience balances intense clarity with warm, scholarly prestige—evoking feelings of mastery, academic sanctuary, and disciplined momentum.

The visual direction merges **Modern Scholarly Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Polishing**:
- Dark-mode and warm ivory layered card architecture that protects against screen fatigue during late-night revision sessions.
- Vibrant golden-amber energy accents representing achievement, intellectual curiosity, and focused energy.
- Crisp, rounded surfaces, micro-glow borders, and pill-shaped metadata badges delivering an inspiring, high-end productivity studio feel.

## Colors

The palette establishes an obsidian academic sanctuary accented with refined golds and warm parchment references:

- **Primary Amber (`#F59E0B`)**: Used for focal calls-to-action, active study mode states, grade milestones, and interactive timers.
- **Secondary Bronze-Amber (`#D97706`)**: Grounded companion for pressed states, key badge outlines, and elevated progress fills.
- **Tertiary Light Amber (`#FDE68A`)**: High-contrast highlight for text accents on dark surfaces, icon accents, and tooltips.
- **Obsidian Neutrals (`#0B0C0E` to `#1E2026`)**: Deep, low-noise dark surfaces that minimize eye strain while rendering golden highlights crisp and vibrant.
- **Ivory Accent Surfaces (`#FAF8F4` / `#EAE4D9`)**: Leveraged for high-contrast card variants or light mode study worksheets that recall premium physical stationery.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes fast scanning, precision metrics, and modern approachability:

- **Primary Sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers friendly, open letterforms with geometric rigor. Headings leverage bold and extra-bold weights with compact negative tracking to feel authoritative yet modern.
- **Monospace Tool Font (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Embedded specifically for academic metrics—Pomodoro countdowns, timetable durations, IGCSE grade boundary calculations, and syllabus module codes. This distinguishes functional quantitative data from narrative instructional copy.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes a fluid single-column model on mobile devices conforming to a 4pt/8pt rhythm, transitioning to a structured 8-column split view on tablets:

- **Mobile Viewports (<640px)**: 16px lateral screen margins with 12px to 16px gaps between interactive study widgets. Cards feature 20px internal padding to provide comfortable touch targets and breathing room for tabular timetable information.
- **Tablet / Split View (>=640px)**: 24px screen margins with dual-pane layout allowing simultaneous inspection of lesson checklists and active Pomodoro countdown tools.
- **Vertical Hierarchy**: Modular card stacking with 12px micro-rhythms between segmented controls and active inputs, and 24px section margins separating discrete tools.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses subtle amber glows and tonal separation to keep the dark aesthetic clean:

- **Base Layer (`#0B0C0E`)**: The canvas surface, representing quiet background focus.
- **Level 1 (`#18191E` with subtle border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`)**: Default container for unselected timetable slots, course topic cards, and settings rows.
- **Level 2 / Active (`#1E2026` with amber edge)**: Highlighted lesson trackers or ongoing revision cards, featuring an inner perimeter border `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)` and an ambient drop shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 16px 0 rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`.
- **Floating Controls & Modals**: Grounded with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and translucent surfaces (`rgba(24, 25, 30, 0.85)`), topped with high-contrast amber CTA outlines.

## Shapes

The interface balances sleek contemporary curves with strict academic utility:

- **Cards and Containers**: Standardized on `rounded-2xl` (16px–20px) to make large dark-mode surfaces feel friendly and accessible.
- **Pills and Badges**: Fully rounded circular radii (`rounded-full`) for status indicators, subject tags (e.g., `MATH 0580`, `PHYSICS 9702`), and progress meters.
- **Controls & Buttons**: `rounded-xl` (12px) for input fields, keypads, and study session triggers to balance ergonomic thumb pressability with structured form alignment.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action**: Amber background (`#F59E0B`), dark obsidian text (`#0B0C0E`), semi-bold typography, subtle amber bottom highlight, and scale reduction on active press (`scale-[0.98]`).
- **Secondary Action**: Translucent dark surface with 1px border (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`), text colored in `#FDE68A`, and hover/press glow.
- **Tactile Tool Buttons**: Segmented mode toggles (e.g., 25m Pomodoro / 5m Break) using pill switches with an active amber slider pill.

### Chips & Subject Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) tags displaying syllabus code in `JetBrains Mono` and title in `Plus Jakarta Sans`.
- Low-saturation background (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.1)`) with gold text (`#F59E0B`) and 1px border.

### Study Tool Widgets
- **Pomodoro Timer**: Circular or arc-based stroke indicator with `#F59E0B` progress gradient, central oversized `label-timer` display, and floating tactile start/pause triggers.
- **Timetable & Lesson Cards**: Modular card (`rounded-2xl`) with vertical accent indicator along the left border showing subject color code, completion checkbox on the right, and exam countdown tag.
- **Grade Calculator Row**: Compact list item pairing subject name, target letter grade pill (A* to U), and numeric weight slider with amber fill track.

### Input Fields & Checkboxes
- **Inputs**: Dark slate fill (`#18191E`), 1px structural border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`), transitioning to amber glow on focus (`outline: 2px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.5)`).
- **Checkboxes**: Custom rounded-md (6px) boxes that fill with vibrant amber and a dark tick icon upon task completion.
