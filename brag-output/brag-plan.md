# Brag Plan: The ANTS

## What is this app?
The ANTS is a curriculum-aware academic productivity and tutoring hub for Myanmar students pursuing Cambridge CAIE and Pearson Edexcel exams — syllabus trackers, grade predictors, timers, and mentor discovery in one amber-accented study system.

## The angle
Not another generic “study app.” Built by top Myanmar exam achievers for the exact boards students sit: Cambridge & Edexcel. The video sells one promise — plan the syllabus, focus deep, predict the grade — and lands on the product’s own punchline: Ace with us.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Giant amber type on slate: “Master Cambridge & Edexcel Exams.” The line that already owns the landing hero — no setup, no joke first. Confidence is the hook.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Brand reveal: The ANTS logo + “Plan, Focus, and Predict Your Grades.”
- Grade Boundary Predictor UI: Raw 168 / 200 → Predicted: A* badge (product’s own preview).
- Curriculum mastery strip: Pure Math 1 (WMA11) progress filling to 82% Done.
- Pomodoro focus chip: 25:00 Focus Block · +20 XP.

## Outro / punchline
Logo lockup. Tagline: Ace with us. Soft CTA whisper: Start Studying for Free.

## User flow worth showing
Open study toolkit → plug raw marks into the Grade Boundary Predictor → Predicted A* lands. (Secondary beat: syllabus % mastery ticking up.)

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet amber academic studio film — confident, Myanmar-specific, never corporate SaaS
- Interpretation: Fewer scenes, longer holds; soft crossfades; JetBrains Mono for numbers; Fraunces/Quicksand energy from the real homepage; restraint over hype.
